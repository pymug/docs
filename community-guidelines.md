*PyMUG Community Guidelines*

Welcome to the Python Mauritius User Group (PyMUG)!

Our community is a space for learning, knowledge sharing, collaboration and connecting with fellow Python and technology enthusiasts.

To keep it welcoming and enjoyable for everyone, please follow these guidelines:

1. Be respectful
Treat everyone with courtesy, regardless of their experience, background or opinions. Personal attacks, insults, harassment and discrimination are not tolerated.

2. Encourage constructive discussions
Technical debates and differing opinions are welcome. Challenge ideas, not individuals. Keep discussions constructive and avoid unnecessary arguments or hostility.

3. Share knowledge and help others
Everyone is here to learn. No question is too basic. Encourage beginners and share your knowledge whenever possible.

4. Keep discussions relevant
Focus on Python, programming, technology, research and related topics. Occasional casual conversations are welcome.

5. Avoid spam and excessive self-promotion
Sharing projects, events, opportunities and useful resources is encouraged. Avoid unsolicited advertising, repetitive promotions and irrelevant content.

6. Respect privacy
Do not share private conversations, personal information or other people’s content without their permission.

7. Respect community moderation
Moderators may intervene, remove inappropriate content or restrict participation when necessary to maintain a welcoming environment.

Above all, let’s keep PyMUG a friendly, inclusive and enjoyable community where everyone feels comfortable learning and contributing. 🚀

Happy coding!
The PyMUG Team
